Inpatient Clinical Syllabus

The 45-Day Hospital Transition Matrix

Engineered exclusively to turn fresh B.Sc./M.Sc. Dietetics and Food Science graduates into hospital-ready practitioners capable of clinical rounds on Day 1.

PHASE 01 • DAYS 1-15 Module 101-104

Clinical Nutrition Foundations & Ward Calculations

Master core hospital bedside diagnostic protocols, decipher medical jargon, and compute baseline nutritional requirements.

  • ICD-10 medical terminology, vitals interpretation & lab sheet decoding
  • BMR, TEE, Mifflin-St Jeor vs. Harris-Benedict stress adjustment factors
  • Anthropometric triage (MAC, Calf Circumference, TSF, Fluid correction)
  • Inpatient Diet Ordering Systems (NBM, Clear Liquid, Soft, Full Fluid)
Milestone Checkpoint Assessment Drill #1

PHASE 02 • DAYS 16-30 Core Inpatient

Therapeutic Diets & Disease-Specific Case Studies

Deep-dive into multi-morbid inpatient care with scheduled 1-on-1 mentor reviews of customized therapeutic formulations.

  • Renal Protocol: Stage 3-5 CKD, Pre-dialysis vs Hemodialysis phosphorus/potassium
  • Diabetic Emergencies: DKA recovery, sliding-scale insulin & glycemic load
  • Cardiovascular & Hepatic Encephalopathy (Sodium, fluid titration)
  • Enteral Tube Feeds (NG/PEG formula rates, osmolarity & refeeding risk)

PHASE 03 • DAYS 31-45 Capstone & Viva

The K-Sheet Capstone Project & Final Clinical Viva

Simulate actual senior dietitian hospital duties. Review unredacted patient charts, defend recommendations, and earn credentials.

  • Complete formulation of 4 live hospital K-Sheets (ICU, Renal, Pediatric, Oncology)
  • Clinical Nitrogen Balance tests & drug-nutrient clash evaluations
  • Live 1-on-1 Viva Defense with Dt. Sk. Reshma simulating hospital consultant interviews
  • Issuance of verifiable Certificate of Clinical Internship with QR Registry ID
