Clarifications for Freshers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything fresh nutrition graduates and early-career dietitians ask prior to enrolling.

How do the 1-on-1 private meeting sessions work? check

Unlike massive webinar programs with 200 participants where you never speak, DietMed pairs you directly into private meeting rooms with Dr. Vikram Kapoor. During these scheduled calls, you share your screen, walk through your patient K-Sheet calculations, receive immediate clinical critique on macronutrient balances, and address personal career concerns.

What is a K-Sheet and why is it essential for hospital jobs? check

A K-Sheet (Kardex Clinical Assessment Sheet) is the official patient-tracking document utilized in tertiary hospital dietetics departments. It contains raw lab panels, edema scores, pharmacological history, anthropometric tracking, and continuous enteral/oral diet charts. In hospital interview rounds, medical heads always test candidates on real K-Sheets. Master them here to easily outperform theoretical applicants.

Can final-year students or career switchers apply? check

Yes. Final-year students pursuing B.Sc, M.Sc, or PG Diplomas in Nutrition and Dietetics are fully eligible to apply. Completing this internship prior to final college graduation allows you to feature hands-on hospital K-Sheet case logs directly on your CV, giving you a competitive edge for clinical hiring upon graduation.

Is the certificate verified and recognized by hiring hospitals? check

Yes. Every DietMed Clinical Academy internship certificate features a verifiable digital QR code and unique Credential ID mapped to our Medical Board Registry. Hospital HR departments and Chief Clinical Dietitians can instantly scan the certificate to verify your 120 completed clinical hours, case-sheet grades, and Dr. Vikram Kapoor's verified sign-off.
