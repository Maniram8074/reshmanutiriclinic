Internship Program

45-Day Clinical Internship

A focused three-phase pathway designed to move learners from clinical foundations into therapeutic case work and a final practical assessment.

All sessions and course delivery are led by Dt. Sk. Reshma.
01Days 1–15

Clinical Nutrition Foundations

Build the base required for clinical dietetics practice.

  • Clinical terminology and patient information review
  • Anthropometric assessment and nutrition calculations
  • Energy and protein requirement calculations
  • Basic inpatient diet planning
02Days 16–30

Therapeutic Diets & Case Studies

Apply nutrition principles to disease-specific scenarios.

  • Renal nutrition and therapeutic restrictions
  • Diabetes and glycaemic management concepts
  • Cardiac and hepatic nutrition considerations
  • Enteral nutrition fundamentals
03Days 31–45

Case-Sheet Capstone & Assessment

Bring the learning together through structured case work.

  • Complete clinical case-sheet exercises
  • Review calculations and therapeutic targets
  • Present and defend clinical reasoning
  • Complete final assessment and documentation
Learning outcome

What you practise during the internship

Clinical reasoning

Learn to organise patient information, identify nutrition priorities and explain the rationale behind your plan.

Therapeutic planning

Practise translating clinical information into structured therapeutic diet recommendations.

Case documentation

Develop familiarity with case-sheet based documentation and presentation.

Mentor feedback

Receive focused guidance from the lead educator throughout the learning journey.
