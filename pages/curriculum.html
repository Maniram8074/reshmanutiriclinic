Curriculum & Mentorship

Structured learning. Direct guidance.

The curriculum combines clinical foundations, therapeutic nutrition, case-sheet practice and one-to-one feedback.

Three learning phases

Each phase builds on the previous one so learners can gradually move from calculations and assessment into complete clinical case presentations.

Phase 01

Assess

Understand the patient story, anthropometry, laboratory data and nutrition priorities.

Phase 02

Plan

Translate assessment findings into energy, protein, fluid and therapeutic diet targets.

Phase 03

Present

Complete a structured case sheet, explain your reasoning and respond to mentor feedback.

Mentorship model

One-to-one learning with the lead educator

Instead of relying only on recorded lessons, learners can use private sessions to discuss their own calculations, case sheets and clinical questions.

01

Prepare

Review the assigned case and gather the required clinical information.

02

Work

Complete calculations and draft the therapeutic nutrition plan.

03

Discuss

Bring your questions and decisions to the one-to-one session.

04

Improve

Apply feedback and strengthen your clinical reasoning.
