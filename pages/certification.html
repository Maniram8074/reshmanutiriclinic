The certification pathway can be organised around completion of course work, clinical case sheets, mentor review and final assessment.

01

Complete modules

Finish the required lessons, exercises and clinical calculations.

02

Submit case work

Complete the assigned case-sheet exercises and practical tasks.

03

Mentor review

Discuss your work with the lead educator and incorporate feedback.

04

Final assessment

Complete the final evaluation and receive course completion documentation.

Certification wording, issuing authority, registration numbers and recognition claims should be added here only after you confirm the exact details you want published.
